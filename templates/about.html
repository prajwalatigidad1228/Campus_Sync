About Campus_Sync

Campus_Sync is a full-stack college management platform with an integrated AI Copilot. Built with Flask, SQLAlchemy, and PostgreSQL, it is designed to be modular, scalable, and hackathon-friendly.

Architecture

  • Backend: Flask (Python) with application factory pattern
  • ORM: SQLAlchemy + Flask-Migrate
  • Database: PostgreSQL (production) / SQLite (development)
  • Frontend: Jinja2 templates + vanilla JS
  • Config: python-dotenv with multi-environment support
